# India privacy and healthcare review: engineering reference

**Reviewed against the linked public sources on 25 November 2026.** This is an engineering research note, not legal advice, a complete legal register, or a compliance assessment. MediAgent is a synthetic-data development build and refuses production mode. An organization considering real use needs independent Indian legal, privacy, security, and clinical review of its actual role, processing purposes, contracts, jurisdictions, deployment, and current law. Recheck official publications, amendments, and implementation dates before any launch; this file is a dated snapshot.

## Official reference points

- The [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) provides for phased commencement. The [13 November 2025 commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) assigns different commencement periods to different provisions. Do not assume all provisions began on the same date.
- The [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), including their [December 2025 corrigendum](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/3c7ebbae0e5456f493f486e6845df86b.pdf), also have phased commencement. Rule 1 specifies publication, one-year, and eighteen-month phases for different rules. Counsel should verify the publication date, any later changes, and the commencement of each provision relevant to the actual deployment. The [MeitY documents page](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025%3B) lists the rules, corrigendum, and enforcement timeline.
- The [National Health Authority's Health Data Management Policy publication page](https://abdm.gov.in/publications/policies_regulations/health_data_management_policy) and [ABDM HMIS/LMIS guidance](https://abdm.gov.in/strapicms/uploads/Guidance_Document_for_ABDM_Compliant_HMIS_LMIS_d066e52a6d.pdf) are starting points if an actual deployment participates in ABDM. The linked guidance discusses, among other things, access control, audit trails, transport protection, data protection, and interoperability. Determine applicability and current versions with NHA and qualified counsel; a general local patient–doctor permission is not an ABDM consent artifact.

## Questions for the deployment review

These are gaps and design questions, not a representation that a particular legal obligation applies or is satisfied.

- **Purpose and notice:** Identify the operating entity and its legal role, all processing purposes, recipients, processors, model providers, and any applicable legal bases. Design reviewed, accessible notices and consent or other workflows for each use. The existing relationship and scoped permission records control application access; they are not a general determination of legal consent or authority for every healthcare situation.
- **Children and guardians:** Determine applicable age, parent/guardian verification, exceptions, and record-access policies. The application does not implement a verified parental or guardian authorization workflow.
- **Security and incident response:** Local Compose uses loopback HTTP, development credentials and local volumes, without a production secrets manager or encryption-at-rest system. Parsing and model prompts are not hardened security boundaries. Establish independently tested access controls, key management, deployment isolation, incident handling, breach assessment and notification procedures, and vendor agreements as applicable.
- **Data rights, retention, and deletion:** Specify procedures and lawful retention periods for requests involving profile information, documents, notes, conversations, audit records, backups, and processor copies. Current document deletion removes access, deletes derived data, and queues removal of the local original or recorded object version; incomplete object discards remain under ongoing reconciliation, but retained notes, conversations, backups, prior downloads, and provider copies may contain information from the document. It is not whole-account erasure. Account deactivation retains records.
- **Provider and transfer review:** OCR runs locally by default; optional Ollama chat, embeddings, and vision send selected data to the configured service. Identify where that service operates, its access, retention and sub-processors, any transfers, and appropriate contractual and technical protections. A completed request cannot be recalled from a provider.
- **ABDM participation and interoperability:** MediAgent does not implement ABHA, an ABDM consent manager, registry verification, FHIR exchange, ABDM sandbox testing, or certification. Select the actual integration role and use current official specifications and onboarding procedures before making an interoperability claim.
- **Operational governance:** Establish a named privacy contact and grievance process, data inventory, access review, retention and deletion controls, independent clinical review, incident exercises, and audit/backup restore reconciliation. No production readiness, clinical safety, legal compliance, or ABDM certification is claimed.
